# Email Tools

- [Resend](https://resend.com/pricing) - Free: 100 emails/day, up to 3,000/month.
- [Postmark](https://postmarkapp.com/pricing) - Free Developer plan: 100 emails/month; no separate daily quota listed.
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) - Free: 300 emails/day; no monthly cap listed (up to 9,000 in a 30-day month).
- [Mailgun](https://www.mailgun.com/pricing/) - Free: 100 emails/day; no monthly cap listed (up to 3,000 in a 30-day month).
- [Amazon SES](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/) - Eligible AWS Free Tier: 3,000 message charges/month for the first 12 months; no daily free quota listed.
- [Mailtrap](https://mailtrap.io/pricing/) - Free Email API/SMTP: 150 emails/day, up to 4,000/month. Email Sandbox is separate and for testing.
