# Observability

- [Dash0](https://www.dash0.com/) - OpenTelemetry-native observability for logs, metrics, and traces. Includes a 14-day full-featured free trial.
- [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) - Error tracking and performance monitoring; free Developer plan for a single user.
- [Grafana](https://grafana.com/) - Open-source dashboards and visualization for metrics, logs, and traces; also available as a hosted free tier via Grafana Cloud.
- [Better Stack](https://betterstack.com/) - Uptime monitoring, incident management, and log management; free tier available.
