# Hosting

- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) - Best for Next.js and React frontends, with serverless functions. Free Hobby plan for personal, non-commercial use.
- [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/) - Best for static sites and Jamstack apps built with Astro, Eleventy, or similar tools. Free plan available with usage limits.
- [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) - Best for static sites and frontend apps; use Pages Functions for lightweight APIs. Free plan available with usage limits.
- [Render](https://render.com/) - Best for backend APIs and web services built with Node.js, Python, Go, or Docker. Free compute plans available with limits.
- [Railway](https://railway.com/) - Best for full-stack apps and APIs that need managed databases or other services. Free plan includes $1/month in usage credits.
- [Fly.io](https://fly.io/) - Best for Dockerized apps and APIs that need globally distributed servers. No ongoing free plan; usage is billed.
- [DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/) - Best for VPS hosting and managed cloud services. No ongoing free plan; introductory credits may be available.
- [Hetzner Cloud](https://www.hetzner.com/cloud/) - Best for low-cost VPS and dedicated servers. No free tier.
- [AWS EC2](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/) - Best for scalable virtual machines and apps integrated with AWS. Free Tier offers depend on account eligibility and have limits.
- [Akamai Cloud (Linode)](https://www.linode.com/) - Best for Linux VPS and cloud applications. Trial credits may be available; ongoing usage is billed.
- [Vultr](https://www.vultr.com/) - Best for VPS, cloud compute, and bare metal servers. Trial credits may be available; ongoing usage is billed.
- [Coolify](https://coolify.io/) - Best for self-hosting and deploying Docker-based apps. The software is free; you provide and pay for the server.
